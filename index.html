Fold 'Em

Card games. Together. Built for iPhone Duo.

Support

Questions, bugs, or ideas for a new game? Email broadsheetapps@gmail.com and include your device and iOS version. You'll get a reply from a person.

Restoring your purchase: open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. The Full Table unlock is shared with your family through Family Sharing.

Flip mode or pass mode: on iPhone Duo, the app uses flip mode on its own. On any other iPhone or iPad it uses pass mode. You can switch in Settings.

Privacy policy

Last updated October 23, 2026.

Fold 'Em does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The app does not connect to any server of ours.

Player names, match history, and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent anywhere, and deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases are handled by Apple. We don't receive your name, email, or payment details. Apple's privacy policy covers those.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
